Overview

Compliant Serialization & Supply Chain Traceability

SAP Advanced Track and Trace is an end-to-end enterprise solution for the pharmaceutical industry. It helps companies comply with drug serialization and reporting requirements. Public authorities and watch agencies leverage advanced track and trace to fight counterfeiting and illicit trade — integrating serialization with processes and offering country-ready reporting packages.

SAP Advanced Track and Trace for pharmaceuticals (ATTP) lowers maintenance costs and helps build a secure supply chain. By leveraging SAP ATTP, our experts help pharmaceutical manufacturers validate products, integrate business processes, and communicate with regulatory bodies — contributing indirectly to patient safety. Our ATTP services are offered on a standalone basis or as an extension to SAP ERP, with special country packs offering protection from ever-changing legislation and regulations.
