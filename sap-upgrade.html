SAP Migration & Upgrades

SAP Migration & Upgrades refers to a major release change, and an update is used when applying EHPs over an existing product, applying support packages, or installing an add-on. The upgrade procedure includes a complex set of tasks and working on almost all ABAP and SAP Java technologies.

Our end-to-end SAP Migration & Upgrades start from the operating system level, move through the database, and culminate with the SAP product. Our chief differentiator is to review and suggest the most common settings. The SAP experts at VKOLLAB Technologies offer secure and reliable business integration services, supporting integration between processes and data, between on-premise and cloud applications, and with other external third-party applications.

By leveraging our custom SAP connector, our developers learn and assimilate quickly — doing away with the need for training, staffing, and other development costs, so they can build and deliver applications that streamline and automate business processes.
