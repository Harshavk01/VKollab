What is SAP IBP?

Integrated Business Planning, Explained

SAP Integrated Business Planning is an intuitive cloud-based solution consisting of tools for analyzing, managing, and changing data to meet logistical challenges more easily. SAP IBP allows you to oversee multiple end-to-end processes simultaneously by combining sales and operations planning with demand planning, inventory management, and purchasing planning.
