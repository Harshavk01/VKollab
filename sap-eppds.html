What It Is

ePPDS: Embedded Production Planning & Detailed Scheduling

ePPDS is a tool embedded within the S/4HANA system that focuses on detailed planning and scheduling, considering resource capacities. It is required for companies needing precise, real-time production planning and control, especially when dealing with complex processes, bottleneck resources, and the need to optimize resource utilization and reduce lead times.
