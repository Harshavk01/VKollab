Overview

Validated, Regulatory-Ready Training Management

ComplianceWire is a globally recognized, cloud-based Learning Management System (LMS) primarily developed and operated by UL Solutions. This proven technology is used globally by pharmaceutical, medical device, and biologics companies to promote public health through training related to the development of safe and effective medicines and medical devices.

It offers robust, validated training solutions that align with FDA and global regulatory standards like 21 CFR Part 11 and EU Annex 11 validation requirements — helping to manage and track employee training, ensure regulatory compliance, and streamline quality processes through a centralized platform for delivering, tracking, and reporting on training activities. Built specifically to support GxP compliance, it helps organizations automate employee training, manage qualification programs, and maintain audit-ready records with features such as electronic signatures, role-based training paths, audit trails, and comprehensive reporting.
