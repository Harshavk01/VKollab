Overview

Cloud-Based Human Experience Management (HXM)

A cloud-based Human Experience Management (HXM) suite that transforms traditional HR into people-centric experiences. SAP SuccessFactors is a leading cloud-based Human Capital Management (HCM) solution that helps organizations manage their workforce with agility, intelligence, and empathy.

Built on the concept of Human Experience Management (HXM), SuccessFactors goes beyond traditional HR, focusing on employee engagement, growth, and productivity. From core HR and payroll to talent acquisition, learning, and performance management, SuccessFactors provides a unified platform that empowers employees and supports organizational success.
